About

I'm a systems and computational neuroscientist, most recently studying the hippocampus and memory. I'm currently a postdoctoral associate with HHMI in Albert Lee's lab, working from Nashville. Before that I worked at Janelia Research Campus, did my PhD through the University of Chicago and Janelia, and started out in barrel cortex and primate optogenetics at Vanderbilt.
