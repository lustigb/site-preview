Who can see this scale?

Method ranges are rough, redrawn after Churchland & Sejnowski (Science 1988) and Sejnowski, Churchland & Movshon (Nature Neuroscience 2014), then extended with newer and molecular-scale methods. Vertical: the smallest detail a method resolves up to the largest region it covers. Horizontal: its finest time resolution up to the longest span it can follow. Scenes are schematic, drawn roughly to scale. Switch between human and rat anatomy at the top. Much of the human physiology shown was first worked out in rats, mice and monkeys.
