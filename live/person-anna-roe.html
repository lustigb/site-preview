How I know Anna

Research mentor, Vanderbilt (2009–12)

I worked with Anna Roe as a research assistant at Vanderbilt after my undergraduate studies there. She was a pioneer of intrinsic signal optical imaging (coming soon), and her work spans vision and touch, studying the cortical column (coming soon) as a common structure across sensory cortex. I learned a great deal from her about the fundamentals of imaging, and I'm grateful for the freedom she gave me to pursue optogenetics with artificial dura and optical windows in monkeys.
