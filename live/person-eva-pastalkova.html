How I know Eva

PhD advisor (2012–16)

Eva Pastalkova was my advisor in graduate school, in the Janelia–University of Chicago Interdisciplinary Scientist Training Program (ISTP). I learned a lot from her, from technique to how to think deeply about questions. I'm grateful for her mentorship, and I wouldn't be where I am today without her.
