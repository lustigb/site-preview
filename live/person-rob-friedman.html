How I know Rob

Mentor; coauthor, VSD imaging (2013)

Rob was a mentor during my time as a research assistant in Anna Roe's lab at Vanderbilt, and a coauthor on our voltage-sensitive dye imaging paper. I'm grateful for his mentorship and for all he taught me about optical imaging.
