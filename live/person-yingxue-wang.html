How I know Yingxue

Collaborator; first author, theta sequences (2015)

I met Yingxue when she was the postdoc, and the only other member, of the Pastalkova lab; she had come to neuroscience from electrical engineering. She was a valuable mentor and coworker during my PhD, and I learned a tremendous amount from her, for which I'm grateful.
