What it is

A deep dive into the board game Blokus: what perfectly symmetric games look like, which symmetries are actually possible, and how to search for them. It started as a plan to train a self-play agent (no AlphaZero-style Blokus agent had been published) and turned into a combinatorics project.

How it works

An exact search solver written in C, plus MATLAB tools for generating, verifying, and visualizing finished games. A "symmetry zoo" catalogs which symmetry types a full four-player game can have.

Interactive animations

The playable pieces from this project, each on its own page. These are the parts closest to ready.

  • Unfold the perfect game: Press play to unfold the four arms of the classic 44-hole game, then click a colour to highlight its quarter.
  • Spot the four rotations: The four arms are the same 89-square shape, just rotated. Click a colour to light up its quarter.
  • Three ways to fold it: Three animations of the same four-fold symmetry: iterative fold, line up and fold, rotate and collapse.
  • Two perfect games, one hole: The original and a newly found perfect game with the identical centre hole, folding in lockstep.
  • Two perfect games: unit and board: Each perfect game as its single repeating arm and as the full board.
  • Perfect-game browser: Browse the four-fold symmetric perfect games, shown as one repeating arm and as the full board.
  • Sealing the hole: One valid starting shell that seals the 44-square centre hole.
