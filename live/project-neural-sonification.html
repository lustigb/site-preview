Hearing the hippocampus

Neural data turned into sound: decoded position becomes pitch, so running sounds like a scale and replay like a quick arpeggio.

neuro · sound · visualization

What it is

Sonification of hippocampal recordings from one session of the TADS task, synchronized with the behavior video. Sonifying the decoded position (rather than individual spikes) gives clean, musical results: a run down an arm is a smooth scale, and a replay event is a rapid arpeggio compressed into a fraction of a second. That makes replay speed something you can hear.
