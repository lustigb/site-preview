What it is

The rat's position over a session is drawn as a 3D trace: the maze floor in two dimensions, and time stretching out along the third. Each neuron's spikes are dots on that trace, so a place cell shows up as a column of dots stacked over the same spot, lap after lap. The animation builds the trace in time, sweeps the camera around it, and plays a tone for each spike.
