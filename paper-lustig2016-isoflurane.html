Oscillatory patterns in hippocampus under light and deep isoflurane anesthesia closely mirror prominent brain states in awake animals

Lustig B, Wang Y, Pastalkova E · Hippocampus 2016

Official version ↗ PDF

In one sentence

Under two different levels of isoflurane anesthesia, the rat hippocampus settles into one of two distinct states, each a close match to one of its well-known waking rhythms.
