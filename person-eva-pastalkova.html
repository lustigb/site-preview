How I know Eva

PhD advisor (2012–16)

Eva Pastalkova was my advisor in graduate school, in the Janelia–University of Chicago Interdisciplinary Scientist Training Program (ISTP). Working with her was a big deal for me.

She is also a natural runner who does neuroscience for fun. Her real passion is the outdoors and running ultramarathons, and she even took on the Barkley Marathons.

Two of her papers are, I would argue, among the most fundamental in the neuroscience literature:

  • Pastalkova E, Serrano P, Pinkhasova D, Wallace E, Fenton AA, Sacktor TC (2006). Storage of spatial information by the maintenance mechanism of LTP. The PKMzeta and memory paper with Todd Sacktor.
  • Pastalkova E, Itskov V, Amarasingham A, Buzsáki G (2008). Internally generated cell assembly sequences in the rat hippocampus. The original episode fields paper with György Buzsáki.

She later left academia, went back to school to become a licensed therapist, and now practices in Colorado.
