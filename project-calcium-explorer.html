Calcium imaging explorer

Explore real calcium-imaging data from thousands of hippocampal neurons, and play with rearranged maps of the same activity.

neuro · data · visualization

Coming soon

What it is

A browser for the open data from Sun et al. (2025): the real cell outlines (ROIs) found by their pipeline and each cell's activity trace. Hover a cell to see its trace, pin cells to compare them, and play the session back.

How the movie is made

The dataset has one image of the field of view, not the raw movie. The movie is simulated: each cell's outline lights up on that image according to its own recorded trace.

Rearranged maps

The fun trick: the explorer can also play the same real traces in rearranged layouts, putting cells with similar activity next to each other to create different topographies. Colour each cell by where on the track it fires and watch the activity play out across the new map.

The data

Sun et al. (2025), Nature, "Learning produces an orthogonalized state machine in the hippocampus" (paper). The data are open (Janelia Figshare, CC BY 4.0).
