What it is

Neuroscales is based entirely on Patricia Churchland and Terrence Sejnowski's original idea and figures, from their 1988 paper Perspectives on cognitive neuroscience (Paper ↗ · PubMed ↗). Their Figure 1 lays out the levels of organization of the nervous system, from the whole CNS down to molecules, and their Figure 3 maps the range of sizes and times each experimental method can measure. This project turns those two figures into something you can zoom through and explore.
